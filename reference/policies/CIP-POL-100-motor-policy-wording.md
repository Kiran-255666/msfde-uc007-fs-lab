## 1. The contract

1.1 Cover is provided only for loss or damage **occurring within the period of cover** shown
on the policy schedule. A loss with a date before the effective date, or after the expiry
date, is **not covered**, whatever the date the claim is reported.

1.2 The policy schedule, this wording and any endorsements form the whole contract.

1.3 Only drivers named on the schedule are covered to drive the insured vehicle, unless the
schedule states otherwise.

## 2. Section A - Damage to your vehicle (Comprehensive only)

2.1 We cover accidental damage to the insured vehicle, including:

- collision with another vehicle or object;
- fire, self-ignition, lightning and explosion;
- theft and attempted theft;
- malicious damage and vandalism;
- **storm, hail, flood and falling objects**.

2.2 We do not cover, under any section:

- wear, tear, rust, corrosion or gradual deterioration;
- mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown;
- damage caused while the vehicle was driven by a person not named on the schedule;
- loss of value after repair, or loss of use;
- damage arising from a deliberate act by the policyholder or any person named on the policy.

2.3 **Third Party, Fire and Theft** policies do not cover accidental collision damage to the
insured vehicle. Cover type is stated on the schedule.

## 3. Excesses

3.1 The collision deductible shown on the schedule is deducted from every collision claim
settlement.

3.2 The glass excess is $100 where the only damage is to glass.

3.3 Where more than one excess applies to the same incident, only the highest is charged.

## 4. Limits

4.1 The most we pay for damage to the insured vehicle is the sum insured shown on the
schedule, or the market value immediately before the loss, whichever is lower.

4.2 Where the cost of repair exceeds **70 percent of market value**, we may declare the
vehicle a total loss and settle on that basis.

## 5. Your duties after a loss

5.1 Tell us as soon as reasonably possible, and in any event **within 30 days** of the
incident.

5.2 **Report theft, attempted theft, malicious damage or vandalism to the police within 5 days
and obtain a crime reference.** We may decline a claim where no crime reference is provided
and the delay has prejudiced our position.

5.3 Provide the documents and information we reasonably require, including photographs of the
damage, a repair estimate and a statement of how the loss happened.

5.4 Do not authorise repairs above $500 before we have agreed them, except to make the vehicle
safe or to prevent further damage.

5.5 Give us accurate and complete information. Providing false or misleading information may
invalidate the claim and the policy.

## 6. Territorial limits

6.1 Cover applies in the United States and Canada. Cover elsewhere requires an endorsement.
