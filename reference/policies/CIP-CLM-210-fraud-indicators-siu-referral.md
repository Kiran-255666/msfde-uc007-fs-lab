# CIP-CLM-210 Fraud Indicators and SIU Referral Guidance

> **FICTIONAL TRAINING DOCUMENT.** Contoso Insurance Group is not a real insurer. This
> content was written for the Microsoft FDE capstone lab.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document ID | CIP-CLM-210 |
| Version | 4.0 |
| Effective date | 2026-05-01 |
| Owner | Special Investigations Unit (SIU) |

## 1. Principle

An indicator is a **reason to look more closely**, never a conclusion. Most claims that show
one indicator are entirely genuine. Staff and automated systems describe what they observed
and refer; they do not allege fraud, and they never decline a claim on suspicion.

## 2. Indicators

### 2.1 Evidence indicators

- The repair estimate is **not consistent with the photographic evidence**, for example panel
  replacement or structural work where the photographs show light cosmetic damage.
- Photographs show damage in a **different area** from the one described in the statement.
- Photographs appear to predate the incident, show a different vehicle, or show pre-existing
  damage.
- No photographs are supplied for damage that is described as significant.

### 2.2 Circumstance indicators

- Damage to an **unattended vehicle** with no witness, no note and no police reference.
- The loss occurs **shortly after cover starts** (within 30 days) or shortly before it ends.
- Notification is **delayed beyond 30 days** without explanation.
- The incident happened in a location with no cameras, at a time with no independent witnesses.

### 2.3 Pattern indicators

- **Three or more claims in 12 months** on the same policy.
- Repeated use of the **same repairer** across unrelated claims by the same policyholder.
- The same third party, witness or repairer appearing across unrelated policyholders.
- The repairer appears on the **enhanced review list** in CIP-CLM-220.

## 3. Referral

3.1 **One or more indicators present** means the claim is referred to the SIU on the **same
day**, using the referral form in the claims system.

3.2 The referral records: the indicators observed, the documents and fields they came from,
and the handler's own observations. It does not record an opinion on whether the claim is
fraudulent.

3.3 The claim is **not** declined, delayed without explanation, or discussed with the
policyholder as a suspected fraud. Normal service standards continue to apply while the SIU
reviews.

3.4 The SIU acknowledges a referral within **2 business days** and gives an initial view
within **10 business days**.

## 4. What an automated system may do

4.1 It may identify indicators, quote the evidence for each, and recommend an SIU referral.

4.2 It must **not** state or imply that a claim is fraudulent, assign a probability of fraud,
decline a claim, or write to the policyholder.

4.3 Its output must be phrased as observation and recommendation, for example: "The estimate
of $6,940 includes door skin replacement and ADAS recalibration, while the supplied
photographs show light scuffing to the door. Recommend referral to the SIU under CIP-CLM-210
section 2.1."
