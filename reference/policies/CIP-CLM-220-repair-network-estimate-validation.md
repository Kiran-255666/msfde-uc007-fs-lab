## 3. Estimate validation rules

3.1 **Operation must match the damage.** Panel replacement is authorised only where the
photographs or an inspection show deformation, tearing or structural damage. Light scuffs,
scratches and shallow dents are repaired and refinished, not replaced.

3.2 **Blending is limited to panels adjacent to the repaired panel**, and to a maximum of two
adjacent panels without a documented colour-match failure.

3.3 **ADAS recalibration** is authorised only where a sensor, camera, radar unit or its
mounting is in the damaged area, or where the windscreen has been replaced.

3.4 **Parts must match the vehicle** recorded on the policy schedule. Part numbers for a
different model, year or trim are rejected.

3.5 **Labour hours** are compared against the published times for the operation. A variance
above **20 percent** requires a written justification from the repairer.

3.6 An estimate that exceeds **$5,000** is referred upward under CIP-CLM-200 section 3.

## 4. Indicative repair values

Used as a sanity check against photographic evidence, not as a settlement figure.

| Damage shown in the evidence | Indicative range |
|---|---|
| Light scuff or scratch, single panel, paint only | $300 - $900 |
| Shallow dent with paint damage, single panel | $600 - $1,600 |
| Bumper replacement with sensors and refinishing | $1,500 - $3,200 |
| Front or rear end collision, multiple panels and lighting units | $3,000 - $8,000 |
| Hail damage, paintless dent repair across several panels | $2,500 - $7,000 |

4.1 Where the estimate sits **outside the indicative range for the damage visible in the
photographs**, record the difference, cite both the estimate line items and the photographs,
and refer under CIP-CLM-210.
